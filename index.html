Everything, in one place

The features that actually save you time.

No bloated dashboards to learn. Every tool here maps to something you already do every week.

🗓️

Flexible weekly scheduling

Publish classes across as many weekdays as you like, edit the whole series any time, and let YogiHub keep the calendar rolling forward automatically.

💳

Secure checkout built in

Students pay by card, iDEAL, Klarna, or PayPal at the moment they book, powered by Stripe. Money lands in your account — YogiHub never touches it.

🔗

One link, fully bookable

A clean public page for your studio that shows real-time availability. Share it in your bio, your newsletter, or a QR code on the studio door.

👥

A client book that remembers everything

Every student, their booking history, lifetime value, and contact details — automatically kept up to date every time someone books.

💬

WhatsApp reminders that land

Send booking and payment reminders straight to WhatsApp, with delivery and read receipts so you know they actually got there.

📈

Earnings you can trust

See expected versus collected revenue by month, so you always know what's actually coming in — not just what's on the calendar.
